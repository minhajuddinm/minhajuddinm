## 🔬 Research & Publications

### [HYPAR: Hyper-Personalized AR Packaging](https://github.com/minhajuddinm/HYPAR-Gen-AI-Texture-XR)
> *Accepted at IEEE ISEMV 2026 (Larnaka, Cyprus, Oct 2026) · First author*

**Paper:** *Designing Hyper-Personalized Retail Product Packaging through Generative AI and Augmented Reality*

A web-based AR system that generates AI textures in real time and overlays them onto physical product packaging using marker-based tracking. Point a camera at a marker on a box and it renders a personalized design on the front face.

- User study: 15 participants, perfume box, SUS 89.50 (SD 5.11)
- Generation latency of 5 to 10 seconds per design (FLUX.1.1 [pro])
- Bachelor's thesis project at SLIDE Studio

### Quantum Key Distribution Research
> *Mitacs Globalink Research Award · University of Aberdeen · May to Aug 2026*
> *Work in Progress track, IEEE CCNC 2027 · Second author*

**Paper:** *Noise-Aware Simulation and Statistical Eavesdropping Detection in BB84, Six-State, and E91 QKD Protocols*

A 12-week international research internship on QKD protocols. The paper compares BB84, six-state, and E91 in Qiskit under noise, with a partial intercept-and-resend attacker and a calibrated CHSH detection threshold based on Hoeffding bounds.

### V.O.I.D.: Single-Arm VR Locomotion
> *Poster, ACM SUI 2026 (Bari, Italy, Oct 2026) · Journal extension under review, IEEE TVCG · Second author*

A study of EFE (Elbow Flexion and Extension), a hands-free locomotion method that maps one arm's vertical hand velocity to forward movement so the other hand stays free for multitasking in VR. A within-subjects study with 40 participants compared EFE with controller and micro-gesture locomotion.

- EFE scored higher than the alternatives on general presence and experienced realism
- Matched controller usability and was faster than micro-gesture

### OSCE Sim: VR Medical Training
Meta Quest app for medical and nursing students, entered in the IEEE Metaverse Reality Grand Challenge (Team Pre Round, with Joel Varghese). An AI patient with a randomly generated condition answers questions, the student orders blood tests, an AI nurse returns results, and the student commits a diagnosis and receives a score. Includes an interactive stethoscope and an LLM-driven diagnostic debrief.

- Pilot study with 7 medical students: mean SUS 86.1 (SD 4.8), confidence gain of +1.31 on a 5-point scale
